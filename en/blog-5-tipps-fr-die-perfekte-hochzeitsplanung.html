5 tips for perfect wedding planning

Planning a wedding requires a lot of time, patience and attention to detail.1. Start early:The best locations and service providers are often fully booked months in advance.2. Set a budget:Be realistic and plan a buffer for unforeseen expenses.3. A common thread:Choose a color concept or theme that runs through the entire wedding - from the invitation to the table decorations.

4. Delegate tasks:You don't have to do everything alone. Rely on professional wedding planners or ask friends for help.5. Enjoy the process:Despite all the stress, don't forget to enjoy the beautiful moments of planning.
