2. Data Collection on the Website

When you visit our website, we or our hosting provider automatically record general server log files (such as IP address, browser type, visited pages, date, and time). This data is technically necessary to display our website to you error-free. The legal basis for this is our legitimate interest (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR). We use cookies only where they are technically necessary for the operation of the site, or where you have explicitly consented.

3. Contacting Us

If you contact us via the form on the website or by email, the data you provide will be stored by us for the purpose of processing the request and in case of follow-up questions. We do not pass on this data without your consent. The legal basis is the performance of pre-contractual measures (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. b GDPR).

4. Data Processing in Event Planning

For the processing and planning of your event, we collect personal data such as names, addresses, contact details, and billing information. Should health-related data (e.g., allergies) be required for catering, we will only process this with your explicit consent (Art. 9 GDPR). The processing is carried out to fulfill the contract.

5. Photo and Video Recordings at Events

At the events we decorate, we sometimes take photos and video recordings of our decorations for PR and marketing purposes (portfolio). This is done on the basis of our legitimate interest (Art. 6 Para. 1 lit. f GDPR). If individuals are recognizable, we obtain their explicit consent in advance.

6. Data Transfer to Third Parties

Your data will only be passed on to third parties if this is necessary for the fulfillment of the contract (e.g., to florists, caterers, or location operators) or if we are legally obligated to do so. We have concluded corresponding data processing agreements (DPA) with our service providers.

7. Storage Duration

We store your data for as long as necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, or as long as legal retention periods exist (in Austria, e.g., 7 years for tax-relevant documents under § 132 BAO).

8. Your Rights

In principle, you have the rights to information, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability, revocation, and objection regarding your data stored by us. If you believe that the processing of your data violates data protection law or your data protection claims have otherwise been violated, you can complain to us (info@selena.events) or the Austrian Data Protection Authority (DSB).
